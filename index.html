06 / About

About Daniel

Hi! I’m Daniel, an esports and gaming journalist who’s been writing about Counter-Strike and the wider games industry since 2022.

Today I’m Counter-Strike Content Lead at Hotspawn, where I interview the scene’s biggest names on camera, run recurring columns like VRS Shift and Roster Radar, and cover the CS2 calendar from Pro League to the Majors. Before that, my time at Esports.net saw me deliver interviews, tournament coverage, evergreen content and news, both on camera and in writing.

Away from esports, I’ve written more than 1,700 articles for GameRant, plus guides, lists and news for Destructoid and CheatCC. That’s timely, high-volume work, and I’ve never let the volume come at the expense of quality.

I also built and run The Gaming Doxie, a gaming TikTok with 21.8K followers and 2.6M likes.

I hold a Master’s in Creative Writing from York St John University, and I’m always open to new opportunities. If you’d like to talk about work, get in touch.
